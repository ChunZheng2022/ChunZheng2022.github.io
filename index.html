Chunzheng Li

I am a Ph.D. student at Soochow University. My research focuses on embodied AI, agent perception, and world models.

I aim to endow agents with multimodal perception and action, enabling them to understand the world and reshape it.

My previous work explores multimodal 3D object detection, few-shot learning, and point cloud perception.

GitHub / ORCID / Publications / Photography

Research

I am interested in how agents perceive, predict, and act in the physical world. My current work studies efficient visual world models for planning, building on my background in multimodal 3D perception.
